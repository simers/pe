## Running Notes / Key Concepts Learned
- **1.1** — PE's core loop: raise outside money → buy private companies → improve over years → sell for more. Leverage (borrowed money) is the return engine. History: ARD/DEC (1940s–50s) → 1980s LBO boom (KKR, Milken junk bonds, RJR Nabisco) → 1990s–2000s institutionalization → 2010s mainstream "alt asset managers" + dry powder → post-2022 higher rates reshape the math.
- **1.2** — Fund = limited partnership. GP (the firm) runs it + commits ~1–5% own capital; LPs (pensions, endowments, sovereign funds, etc.) are passive, supply ~95%+, liability limited to commitment. Capital is *committed* then *called* (drawdowns) over a ~5yr investment period; ~10yr fund life. Economics = "2 and 20": ~2% management fee (steady, paid regardless of performance) + 20% carried interest (profit share, the alignment driver). Distribution waterfall order: (1) return of capital → (2) preferred return / hurdle (~8%) → (3) GP catch-up → (4) 80/20 split. J-curve = early negative returns (fees, no exits yet) then positive as companies are sold.
- **Side topic — J-curve, real funds:** Used CalPERS public PE disclosures (as of 9/30/2025) to show the J-curve via same-manager funds at different ages (Clearlake III/V/VII; Silver Lake IV/V/VI). Young 2022 funds (e.g., Arsenal VI −3.6%, Tiger Global XV −5.9%) sit underwater in the trough = normal, not failure. Interim IRRs noisy; CalPERS flags vintage 2021+ as "not meaningful."
- **Side topic — return metrics (DPI/IRR):** Two families. MULTIPLES (ignore time): DPI = cash returned ÷ paid-in (realized, in-hand); RVPI = unrealized value still held ÷ paid-in (paper marks); TVPI = DPI + RVPI = total value ÷ paid-in (= CalPERS "Investment Multiple"). MOIC = deal-level cousin (total value ÷ invested). IRR (all about time) = annualized rate setting NPV to zero; very timing-sensitive (2× in 3yrs ≈ 26% IRR vs 2× in 7yrs ≈ 10%). Multiples + IRR can diverge (Clearlake III 2.88×/40.7% vs Silver Lake IV 2.78×/20.9% — same TVPI but DPI 2.86× vs 1.81×: Silver Lake leans on unrealized RVPI, so TVPI is the misleading one there, not IRR). "DPI is the new IRR." IRR can be flattered by subscription lines. Revisited in 1.6 (hurdle = IRR threshold) and 5/8 (compute by hand).
- **1.3** — PE is one neighborhood in ALTERNATIVES (everything beyond public stocks/bonds). LPs use alts for: illiquidity premium, diversification, access to private economy. Map by two axes: (a) company life-cycle equity continuum = Venture (startups, minority, no debt, power law) → Growth (scaling, proven model) → Buyout/LBO (mature, control, leveraged = the "PE" this course means); (b) capital structure = private credit/direct lending sits ABOVE equity (paid first, contractual interest, lower risk) — often the debt in a buyout deal. Real assets = real estate + infrastructure (stable, long-life, lower risk/return). Hedge funds = structural outlier: liquid, public-market, open-ended, NO capital calls/J-curve/DPI. Terminology trap: "PE" broad = VC+growth+buyout; "PE" narrow/colloquial = buyout. LPs set strategic asset allocation across the map; key constraint = illiquidity budget (must keep enough liquid to pay obligations). Mega-firms (Blackstone, KKR, Apollo, Carlyle, Ares) now span the whole map = why "alternative asset managers."
- **1.4 (current state, searched ~Jun 2026)** — EM PE breaks the developed-market assumptions. Key structural differences: (1) leverage often unavailable (shallow debt markets) → EM PE = minority GROWTH equity, not leveraged buyout; returns from growth not financial engineering. (2) CURRENCY RISK is the defining hazard: LP measures in USD, company earns local — depreciation erodes $ returns (2× local × 0.5 FX = ~1.0× USD; 2.5× × 0.6 = 1.5×). (3) Exits harder/slower (shallow IPO mkts, fewer buyers) → #1 investor concern; reliance on trade sales (+60% YoY APAC), secondaries (~25% of deals), continuation funds; longer holds. (4) Political/regulatory/rule-of-law risk (capital controls, expropriation, China govt funds pushing "guaranteed returns"). (5) Weaker data/audited financials. (6) Family/founder-owned targets reluctant to cede control. Bull case = growth/demographics. India = current standout (most-cited best EM in Preqin 2024/25; >⅓ of new Asian PE capital; $78B since 2020; demographic tailwind). Headline = rotation away from China toward India. Global backdrop: fundraising down 3rd straight yr (~$480B), flight to quality/mega-funds, "12 is the new 5" (cheap debt + multiple expansion era over). Key judgment: a HURDLE RATE prices risk (an IRR threshold for sharing upside) but does NOT control risk — currency/capital-controls/exit risk need structure, hedging, political-risk insurance (MIGA/DFC), treaty jurisdictions, pre-lined-up exits. Higher hurdle can even misalign incentives (GP zeroed out → swings for fences).
- **1.5** — The strategy toolbox INSIDE PE, organized by maturity / health / transaction type / whether you even buy an operating company. Buyout playbooks: (1) BUY-AND-BUILD (platform + add-ons) = dominant modern strategy; engine is MULTIPLE ARBITRAGE — buy small firms at low multiples, re-rate at scale (worked ex: platform $20M EBITDA@8× + $15M add-ons@5× = $35M for $235M; exit @10× = $350M → ~$115M created before any organic growth). (2) CARVE-OUT = buy non-core division from a corporate; challenge = disentanglement → TSA (Module 2). (3) TAKE-PRIVATE = buy + delist a public co (large, equity-heavy, drives megadeals). (4) TURNAROUND/DISTRESSED/special situations = fix broken/over-levered cos; distressed-for-control = buy debt cheap, convert to equity via restructuring; counter-cyclical. Non-operating strategies: (5) SECONDARIES = buy existing fund stakes (LP-led / GP-led continuation funds); buying a mature fund SKIPS the J-curve trough → shallower/shorter J-curve, faster DPI. (6) Co-investment (direct, fee-free, alongside GP) + fund-of-funds (diversified, extra fee layer). Cross-cutting: THREE VALUE-CREATION LEVERS = financial engineering (leverage) / multiple arbitrage / operational improvement (grow EBITDA). Industry shifted decisively toward OPERATIONAL (cheap-debt + multiple-expansion tailwinds gone). Sets up Modules 5–7.
- **1.6** — LBO return engine = thin equity + lots of debt; 3 levers grow equity (Equity = EV − Net Debt): EBITDA growth, debt paydown (deleveraging; $1 repaid = +$1 equity), multiple change. Sources & Uses: EV = Debt + Equity at entry. Illustrative: $100M EBITDA@10× = $1,000M EV, 50/50 financed; 5yr → EBITDA $150M, debt $500→$300M, exit 10× → exit equity $1,200M = 2.4× MOIC / ~19% IRR. MOIC↔IRR↔time table (2×: 26/15/10% over 3/5/7yr; 3×: 44/25/17%). Exit multiple = biggest swing (8/10/12× → 1.8/2.4/3.0×). Leverage amplifies both ways (70/30 → 3.3×/27%, but equity can zero out). Hurdle = IRR threshold (8% pref must clear before carry). REAL ANCHOR: Blackstone/Hilton (2007–18): $26B (~$20.5B debt + $5.6B equity), held through crisis, asset-light franchising + new CEO, ~$14B profit, ~3× MOIC, ~16% IRR/11yrs (most profitable LBO ever; great multiple + long hold = modest IRR; survival = strategy). Counter: TXU/Energy Future Holdings (~$45B, 2007, largest LBO ever) → 2014 bankruptcy, ~−100%.
- **1.7** — Leverage sized in "turns of EBITDA" (Total Debt ÷ EBITDA). Current mkt (verified): entry ~11–12× EV/EBITDA, leverage ~4–5× (down from 6–7× pre-2022), debt cost ~9–11%, equity ~45–55% (up) → more reliance on operations. Higher rates cap debt via interest coverage (EBITDA/interest, want 2×+). DEBT STACK (paid first/cheap → last/expensive): senior secured 1st lien (revolver + TLB, floating SOFR+spread) → 2nd lien → senior unsecured/high-yield → subordinated/mezzanine (PIK, warrants) → equity (first loss, uncapped upside). Constraints: max leverage, min coverage, min equity; covenants = maintenance (quarterly) vs incurrence (on action); "cov-lite." Leverage CREATES value 4 ways: amplification, interest tax shield (deductible), discipline, deleveraging. Risks: fixed interest → distress/bankruptcy; floating-rate exposure; refinancing walls. REAL ANCHOR: Verint ~$2.7B leveraged loan (refis Calabrio too), Verint EBITDA ~$249.5M, Calabrio debt = SOFR+ unitranche held by private credit (Golub/HPS/Monroe).
